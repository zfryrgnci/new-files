## Full description (front-loaded with search keywords)
Streak is a simple, beautiful habit tracker that helps you build good habits and
break bad ones — one day at a time. Tap to check in, watch your streak grow, and
never break the chain.

Whether you want to drink more water, read daily, work out, meditate, quit smoking,
or study consistently, Streak keeps your daily habits and routines on track.

WHY STREAK
• One-tap daily check-ins — fast and satisfying.
• 🔥 Streak counter — don't break the chain.
• Weekly dots + a full history heatmap for every habit.
• Current streak, best streak, and 30-day completion rate.
• Beautiful, distraction-free design. Works fully offline.
• Your data stays on your device.
• Share your streak to keep yourself accountable.

STREAK PRO (subscription)
• Unlimited habits (free plan includes 3)
• Extra color themes
• Remove all ads
• Detailed stats & history
Monthly or yearly. Cancel anytime in Google Play.
