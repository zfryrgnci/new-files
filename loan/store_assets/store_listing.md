## Full description
Loan Calculator makes it easy to work out any loan in seconds. Enter the amount,
interest rate, and term — and instantly see your monthly payment (EMI), total
interest, and a full year-by-year payment schedule.

Perfect for home loans, mortgages, car loans, personal loans, and student loans.

FEATURES
• Instant EMI / monthly payment calculation.
• Total interest and total amount paid.
• Principal vs interest breakdown.
• Full year-by-year amortization schedule.
• Adjust with simple sliders or type exact values.
• Any currency. Works fully offline. No sign-up.

Note: This is a calculator for estimates. It does not provide loan offers or connect
to any bank.
