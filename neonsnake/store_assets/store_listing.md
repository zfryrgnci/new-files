## Full description
Neon Snake Rush is the classic snake game reborn in glowing synthwave style.

Swipe to steer your neon serpent, devour pulsing food, and grab rare coin drops to
grow longer and faster. Bank your coins between runs to buy permanent upgrades — start
longer, or multiply every coin you collect. One wrong turn ends the run… unless you
cash in a revive.

FEATURES
• Smooth one-thumb swipe controls.
• A coin economy with permanent upgrades (Start Length, Coin Multiplier).
• Rare coin food worth 5x — risk it for the reward.
• Rewarded "revive" to save a great run.
• Slick neon glassmorphism visuals and particle juice.
• Saves your progress. Plays offline. Tiny download.
• Optional one-time "Remove Ads".

Simple to learn, addictive to master. How long can your snake grow?
