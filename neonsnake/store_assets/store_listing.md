## Full
Neon Snake Rush is the classic snake game reborn in glowing synthwave style. Swipe
to steer your neon serpent, eat to grow, grab rare coin drops, and bank coins for
permanent upgrades. One wrong turn ends the run — unless you cash in a revive.
Features: smooth one-thumb controls; on-screen D-pad; coin upgrades (start length,
coin multiplier); rewarded revive; neon visuals; saves progress; plays offline;
optional one-time Remove Ads.
