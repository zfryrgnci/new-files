## Full description
Quantum Ascent is a fast, floaty vertical platformer with buttery physics and a
neon-noir soul.

Guide your glowing orb upward through 12 hand-tuned levels across three worlds — The
Grid, The Core, and The Void. Collect three data cores to open the exit portal, grab
coins, and dodge glitching hazards. Clear milestones to permanently unlock new powers:
the DOUBLE JUMP and the QUANTUM DASH, which reshape how you climb.

FEATURES
• Silky, forgiving physics — floaty jumps, natural bounces, precise control.
• 12 escalating levels across 3 themed worlds.
• Permanent power unlocks: Double Jump and Quantum Dash.
• Data cores, coins, checkpoints and a rewarded revive.
• Saves your progress. Plays offline. Tiny download.
• Optional one-time "Remove Ads".

Easy to pick up, satisfying to master. Can you reach Ascension?
