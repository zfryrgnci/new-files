## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Space Bala is a fast, neon-soaked retro space shooter built for one-thumb play.

Pilot your fighter through escalating waves of enemies — basic raiders, lightning
interceptors, armored asteroids, and weaving phantom ships — then face the pulsing
neon BOSS in a final showdown. Every run is a fresh sprint for a new high score.

FEATURES
• One-thumb touch controls — move and fire, that's it.
• Five escalating levels, each with new enemy types.
• A multi-phase neon boss battle with a real victory screen.
• Three lives with a fair respawn, so every run counts.
• Slick synthwave visuals and glowing particle explosions.
• Plays instantly — no login, no waiting, tiny download.
• Optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase.

Simple to pick up, tough to master. How far can you push the wave counter before
the boss ends your run?
