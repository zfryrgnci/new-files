## Full description
Money Manager is a simple, private expense tracker that helps you take control of
your money. Log income and spending in seconds, set a monthly budget, and see
exactly where your money goes.

FEATURES
• Fast add — amount, category, note, date.
• Monthly income / expense / balance at a glance.
• Set a monthly budget with a progress bar and over-budget warning.
• Category spending reports with percentages.
• Beautiful, distraction-free design. Works fully offline.
• Your financial data stays on your device — no account, no cloud.

MONEY PRO (subscription)
• Remove all ads
• Full reports & charts
• Custom themes
• Unlimited history
Monthly or yearly — cancel anytime.

Perfect for daily expense tracking, budgeting, saving money, and managing personal
finance. Start tracking today.
